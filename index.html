RoboDust is a robot vacuum for your Mac desktop. It maps the mess, sucks up your files one by one, and drives them back to its dock. Nothing really changes until you say so.

It’s free! Works for Macs on macOS 14 or later.

Is it free?

Yes, with no account and no ads. If you’d like to help me make more of these, there’s a way to chip in at the bottom of this page.

Can I see the code?

Yes, it’s all on GitHub under the MIT license. Good for an app that deletes things. Ideas and fixes are welcome too, and I read everything.

Does it really delete my files?

Only if you pick Delete and press the button. It can’t be undone, so I’d start with Trash. The sample mess never touches anything real.

Which Macs does it run on?

Apple silicon and Intel, on macOS 14 or later. It cleans your main display for now.

Why does it ask for Finder and Desktop access?

It reads where your icons sit and what they look like, so it can draw them in exactly the same places. Finder also does the moving when you choose Trash or Tidy up. The sample mess needs neither.

What if my Desktop syncs with iCloud?

It works, and it warns you first: anything you delete also disappears from your other devices, the same as if you’d done it yourself.

What does it know about me?

Your file names, sizes and thumbnails, only on your Mac, and only to draw them. They never leave it. The one thing it asks the internet is whether there’s a new version.

Your desktop is a mess. It’s fine. Everyone’s is.
